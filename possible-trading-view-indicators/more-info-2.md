## 4. The Arbitrage Strategy Explained

### 4.1 How It Works

1. **Data Source Monitoring**
   - Bot monitors live BTC/ETH prices on Binance and Coinbase
   - Detects confirmed directional momentum

2. **Latency Exploitation**
   - Polymarket's 15-minute crypto markets update slower than major exchanges
   - During this lag (seconds), the odds become "stale"

3. **Entry Mechanism**
   - When YES + NO contract prices sum to less than $1.00 (should always equal $1.00)
   - Bot buys the "mispriced certainty" - typically at 95-97¢
   - Sometimes even lower during liquidity gaps

4. **Profit Mechanics**
   - No directional prediction required
   - Simply captures the arbitrage between confirmed reality and lagging odds
   - High frequency (400+ trades/week) with small, consistent gains

### 4.2 Why This Works

```
Risk-Free Arbitrage Formula:
- If BTC clearly moving UP on Binance (confirmed)
- And Polymarket "BTC Up" contract = $0.95
- And Polymarket "BTC Down" contract = $0.03
- Total = $0.98 (should be $1.00)
- Buy "BTC Up" at $0.95, resolve at $1.00 = $0.05 profit
- Repeat thousands of times = compounding returns
```

## 5. Polymarket Platform Context

### 5.1 What is Polymarket?
- Blockchain-based prediction market platform
- Users trade shares tied to probability of future events
- Outcomes settled on-chain
- Markets include politics, crypto prices, sports, current events

### 5.2 15-Minute Crypto Markets
- Short-duration "Up or Down" predictions
- Resolve every 15 minutes
- Available for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP
- High-frequency trading environment

| Fee Attribute | Details |
